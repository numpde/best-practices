Research method and evidence limits
===================================
26 October 2026

The graph combines a public repository-practice survey with reviewed editorial
synthesis from broader engineering research. It distinguishes patterns from
anti-patterns, conceptual scope from importance, and causal relationships from
mere topic similarity.

The 38 original public criteria are embedded in the graph with their source
references. Examples are scoped: adapting an underlying obligation does not
make every scientific mechanism universal. Original source wording is retained
as evidence, rather than presented as a fresh runtime result.

Additional synthesis
--------------------
Sixteen research summaries cover deterministic caches and randomness,
diagnosis before experiment changes, comparable metrics, experiment machinery,
controlled updates, training fit, high-error populations, configuration
adaptation, scientific limitations, reader understanding, principled common
standards, maintained qualification, reproducible measurements, purposeful
preservation, stable approval, and discovery at the existing owner.

The public page contains reviewed summaries and generic examples. It does not
publish private source conversations, machine identities, local filesystem
locations, session IDs, or private repository provenance. These summaries are
editorial recommendations; they are not publicly reproducible quotations from
the underlying private research. Do not treat them as independent proof of a
particular implementation's behavior.

Relationships
-------------
Each edge states a directional relationship and the reason for it. Connections
are synthesis judgments. Being connected does not establish logical equivalence
or imply that one rule applies without the scope stated by the other.

Implementation, tests, scientific qualification, deployment, and live proof
remain distinct evidence. Historical source material is not a current runtime
qualification. Source and browser checks are described in the graph-design note.
