Examples and editorial review
==============================

Every graph item has at least three illustrative examples: its original example
plus at least two privacy-edited additions. The first research pass added 148
examples across the original 74 concepts. A further 20 examples integrate
recent engineering cases, including two added anti-pattern concepts. The graph
now has 76 concepts and 242 examples.

The examples were synthesized from criteria, repository changes, and Codex
discussions. They are not verbatim quotes or claims that each scenario happened
exactly as described. Private source paths, hostnames, account details, and
session identifiers are omitted. A small number link to public commits that
directly support the adjacent point; links were omitted where the source
included private workstation details or did not support the wording closely
enough.

The editorial review checked each example against its item's title, summary,
and scope. It checked for a concrete starting condition and action, a
consequential result where relevant, anti-patterns that name the harmful
behavior, and claims no broader than the source supports. Search includes all
examples.

The recent engineering examples cover classified retry and uncertainty,
backpressure at feed admission, capacity held through streamed responses,
callback-safe cleanup joins, interface-bound network acquisition, exact-byte
admission from shared caches, private diagnostic redaction, and separation of
quote cleanup time from payment acceptance state. Their limits remain explicit:
the network proxy example is scoped to its documented IPv4/cooperative routing
model; redaction tests cover recognized forms, not every arbitrary secret; and
a purchase cleanup clock does not establish the payment authority's acceptance
state. These are illustrative design cases, not claims of complete cross-service
qualification.

The examples illustrate recommendations; they do not establish that a current
implementation follows them or that a test has passed. Their evidence limits
remain those stated in each item and in the research method note.

Reader validation
-----------------
A context-isolated reader teach-back was not available for this update. The
editorial and source-aware review is not a substitute for independent reader
validation, so clarity has not been independently established. The examples
are compact by design and retain some field-specific vocabulary for an
engineering audience.
