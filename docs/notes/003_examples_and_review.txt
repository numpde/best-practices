Examples and editorial review
==============================

Every graph item has three illustrative examples: the original example retained
from the first edition, plus two privacy-edited additions. The 148 additions
were written as short, concrete illustrations of the criteria and findings
collected from repository changes and Codex discussions. They are not verbatim
quotes or a claim that each scenario occurred exactly as described. A small
number link to public commits that directly support the adjacent point; links
were omitted where the source included private workstation details or did not
support the wording closely enough.

The additions were reviewed against each item's title, summary, and scope. The
review checked that practice examples describe an actor, action, and outcome;
anti-pattern examples show the harmful behavior or its consequence; scientific
examples preserve distinctions such as prediction versus measurement; and
claims do not exceed the stated scope. Search includes all three examples.

The examples illustrate the recommendations; they do not establish that a
current implementation follows them or that a test has passed. Their evidence
limits remain those stated in each item and in the research method note.

Reader validation
-----------------
A context-isolated reader teach-back was not available for this update. The
editorial and source-aware review is not a substitute for independent reader
validation, so clarity has not been independently established. The examples
are compact by design and retain some field-specific vocabulary for an
engineering audience.
